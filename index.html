About UKBD

A platform for international connections

UKBD World Trades PLC aims to bring together people and organisations interested in education, recruitment, trade and cross-border business relationships.

Our mission is to create opportunities through technology, ethical business and virtual collaboration. We welcome students, healthcare workers, professionals and partners who share our commitment to equal opportunity and supporting communities.
